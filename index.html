Getting started

Your first step is a phone call

Reaching out is often the hardest part. Here is exactly what happens when you do — no surprises, no pressure.

Step one

Call the office

Call (281) 413-8063 and tell us, in your own words, what’s going on. We’ll talk through what kind of session fits and find a time that works.

Step two

Meet your counselor

Your first session is a conversation, not an interrogation. You set the pace, share what you’re ready to share, and ask every question you have.

Step three

Move forward, together

You’ll leave with a clear sense of the path ahead — practical next steps for the week, and a plan you actually believe in.

Our approach

Professional care, Christian foundation

Faith isn’t an add-on here — it’s woven into how we understand people, marriage, family, and change. And it’s always at your pace: as much or as little as you want in the room.

  • Licensed, evidence-based counseling methods
  • Scripture and prayer available in session, at your lead
  • Completely confidential — what’s said here stays here
  • Practical homework between sessions, not just talk
