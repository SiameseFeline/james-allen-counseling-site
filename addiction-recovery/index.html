About this service

What working together looks like

Addiction counseling offers a confidential, judgment-free path to recovery. It is led by a Licensed Chemical Dependency Counselor — someone trained specifically in this work, not a generalist guessing at it.

The focus is breaking free from alcohol, drugs, and other destructive patterns — and then rebuilding life on the other side of them. That rebuilding is the longer work, and it is where practical next steps between sessions matter most.

Help is available for individuals and for families affected by addiction. What is shared in session stays in session, protected under Texas law and professional licensing standards, with the standard exceptions involving safety — which your counselor will explain clearly at your first visit.

Getting started

Step one — call the office. Call (281) 413-8063 and tell us, in your own words, what’s going on. We’ll talk through what kind of session fits and find a time that works.

Step two — meet your counselor. Your first session is a conversation, not an interrogation. You set the pace, share what you’re ready to share, and ask every question you have.

Step three — move forward, together. You’ll leave with a clear sense of the path ahead — practical next steps for the week, and a plan you actually believe in.

Faith isn’t an add-on here — it’s woven into how we understand people, marriage, family, and change. And it’s always at your pace: as much or as little as you want in the room. Sessions are completely confidential, and the practical work continues between sessions — not just talk.

Serving the area: James Allen & Associates counsels clients from The Woodlands, Spring, Conroe, Tomball, and Magnolia. The office is on Oak Ridge Drive in The Woodlands — easy to reach from across the area. Call ahead and we’ll have a time set aside for you.
