About this service

Space for what is heavy

Grief is not only about death — it can follow divorce, diagnosis, or the loss of a future you expected. Trauma can leave the nervous system on alert long after.

Sessions move at a pace that does not retraumatize. James’s clinical and pastoral background is especially relevant here.

Who this is for

  • Adults processing death of a loved one
  • People adjusting after divorce or major health news
  • Those still affected by past trauma or shock
