About this service

Stability is the goal — not perfection

Counseling complements psychiatric care. We focus on early warning signs, routines that protect stability, and the impact on work and relationships.

Who this is for

  • Adults with a bipolar diagnosis seeking counseling alongside medication
  • People whose mood and energy swings disrupt life
