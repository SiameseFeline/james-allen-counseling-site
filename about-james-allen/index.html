About James Allen

Clinical care and faith, in the same room

James Allen understands clinical work — and he understands faith, church life, and the pressure of leading while hurting. That means you never have to choose between sound professional care and counsel that respects your beliefs. Both are in the room, in every session.

He holds a Master of Education in Counseling and a Bachelor of Arts in Theology and Sociology from Houston Baptist University, and completed an executive management program at Harvard. His counseling practice covers marriage and family, ADHD, addictions, anger management, and conflict resolution — as well as divorce recovery, grief and loss, depression, anxiety, child discipline, and financial stress.

Alongside his counseling work, James speaks to schools, churches, and civic organizations on family life, faith, and working through hard seasons well. There is no speaking fee, though an honorarium is customary. To ask about a speaking engagement, call the office at (281) 413-8063.
