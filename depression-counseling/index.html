About this service

Care that takes the weight seriously

Depression is not a character flaw or a lack of faith. Counseling starts with your story — what you have tried, what is in the way, and what a better week would look like.

James brings licensed counseling and 20+ years of pastoral ministry. Faith can be part of the conversation if you want it — never as a requirement.

Who this is for

  • Adults with persistent sadness, emptiness, or disconnection
  • People functioning at work but exhausted doing it
  • Those recovering from loss, burnout, or relational strain
